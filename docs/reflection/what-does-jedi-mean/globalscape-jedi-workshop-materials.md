# GlobalScape JEDI Workshop Materials

The GlobalScape JEDI in Science Communication workshop was developed by Jon Chase (Leiden University) and colleagues as a practical, facilitated introduction to justice, equity, diversity and inclusion for science communicators from different regions of the world. It explains what JEDI means and how it applies in practice, drawing on recent research and inviting participants to share experiences with one another.

The materials are available openly and can be adapted to a citizen science or citizen observatory context. They include a Facilitator's Guide, a Workbook, and a Slide Deck, making them suitable for use in team workshops or training sessions. Recordings of the workshop in two parts are also available on YouTube.

SOURCE: Chase, J., Russo, P., Roche, J. (2023). _GlobalScape Workshop Materials: Justice, Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (JEDI) in Science Communication_. Zenodo. [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7764990](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7764990)
