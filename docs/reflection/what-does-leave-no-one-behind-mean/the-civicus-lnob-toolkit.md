# The Civicus LNOB Toolkit

The Leave No One Behind Toolkit was developed by CIVICUS, Development Initiatives, and Project Everyone — the founding partners of the Leave No One Behind Partnership — as a practical resource for civil society organisations, coalitions, groups, and individuals seeking to understand and act on the commitment to leave no one behind that lies at the heart of the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The toolkit provides accessible guidance on understanding the SDGs and the LNOB pledge, developing advocacy and campaigning strategies, organising national dialogues and consultations, and identifying who is being left behind and why. It is structured around three core principles: **Examine** (understanding who is left behind and where), **Engage** (creating compelling campaigns and advocacy), and **Empower** (enabling marginalised people and civil society to speak for themselves and hold governments to account).

SOURCE: CIVICUS, Development Initiatives & Project Everyone (2016). _Leave No One Behind Toolkit_. Leave No One Behind Partnership. [www.leavenoonebehind.global](http://www.leavenoonebehind.global)
