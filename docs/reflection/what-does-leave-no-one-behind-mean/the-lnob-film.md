# The LNOB Film

The _Leave No One Behind_ film was created by the Leave No One Behind Partnership — comprising CIVICUS, Development Initiatives, and Project Everyone — to bring the LNOB concept to life. Using scenes from Yann Arthus-Bertrand's film _HUMAN_, it gives voice to some of the world's most marginalised people and makes a compelling case for why ensuring that no one is left behind matters. It was launched in front of world leaders at the UN General Assembly in September 2016 and is available in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and German.

This short film is a powerful starting point for introducing the LNOB pledge to a team, community group, or audience unfamiliar with the concept. It can be used to open a workshop or dialogue session, shared on social media to raise awareness, or screened at events as a discussion prompt.
