# The Energy Diagnosis Chart

The Energy Diagnosis Chart was developed in response to the needs expressed by Citizen Observatories participating in the CitiObs project as front-runner and fellow cases,  to support them in understanding and strengthening their participation models. It provides a participatory diagnostic tool that maps the vitality and sustainability of a citizen science initiative or community project across 12 dimensions of health, organized into four domains: _Task Effort & Support_, _Team & Social Connection_, _Growth & Continuity_, and _Impact & Outcomes_.

**What It's For**

To surface how "alive" and sustainable an initiative feels in real time—not through external evaluation, but through the eyes of the people running it. The radar reveals which areas are thriving (close to 5) and which feel stuck or draining (closer to 0), offering a non-judgmental snapshot of initiative health.

**Key Features**

* **12 interconnected dimensions** (e.g., task difficulty, team coordination, leadership renewal, policy engagement, data use)
* **0–5 scale** on each spoke: from deficit ("difficult/complex") to strength ("easy/well-supported")
* **Visual profile**: Connecting the dots creates a shape that groups can immediately "read"—spiky, lopsided, or well-rounded
* **Action companion**: A second page ("From Energy to Action") turns diagnosis into strategy, asking _what to change, with whom, and how_

**Workshop Use**

1. **Shared Sense-Making** (\~20 min): Individual or small-group mapping to generate conversation about initiative realities and contextual factors, and share insights with each other
2. **Pattern Spotting** (\~15 min): Compare shapes across groups or time. Does everyone see the same bottlenecks? What surprises?
3. **Systems Thinking**: Use mismatches to surface interdependencies—e.g., "We have great social connection but no impact visibility—that's demotivating."
4. **Action Planning**: Move directly from "what's stuck?" to "what can we shift?"—and with whom—using the second page
