# Diversci Spark — Self-Evaluation Workshop

The Diversci Spark tool is a facilitated workshop for collective reflection and internal self-evaluation on equity, diversity and inclusion. Developed by Diversci — an international collective of science engagement professionals — it has been used over more than five years to help organisations have structured, yet honest conversations about EDI and to measure their institutional knowledge and practices regarding social justice. Spark is not simply a diagnostic exercise: it is designed as a means of committing to and implementing change.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The workshop guides teams through Diversci's five transversal pillars — **Access, Content, Partners, Staff, and Strategy** — asking participants first to self-evaluate their organisation's current level of awareness and practice, and then to plan a roadmap for change using a Stop / Start / Continue exercise. It is suitable for groups of up to 25 people and can be run online or in person in approximately two hours.

SOURCE: Diversci (n.d.). _Spark: Self-Evaluation Workshop_. [https://www.diversci.eu/evaluation-workshop/](https://www.diversci.eu/evaluation-workshop/)
