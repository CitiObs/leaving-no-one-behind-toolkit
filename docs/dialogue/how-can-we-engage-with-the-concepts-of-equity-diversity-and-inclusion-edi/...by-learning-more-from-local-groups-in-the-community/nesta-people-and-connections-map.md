# NESTA People and Connections Map

The People & Connections Map is a quick and visual tool for mapping exactly who you are trying to reach and how. Developed by Nesta as part of the Development Impact and You (DIY) toolkit for social innovation, it provides an overview of all the different individuals and organisations involved in your work, and allows you to develop a clearer picture of how they relate both to your work and to each other. This might include the communities you work directly with, funding bodies, peer organisations, and local or international support networks.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The tool uses concentric circles to indicate proximity to the target audience — with those closest to the core representing the most local relationships, and those towards the outside representing more distant ones. People and organisations can also be clustered into sections representing specific networks, sectors, or interest areas. Once completed as a team, the map provides a useful starting point for discussing which relationships are key and which may need more attention — making it particularly valuable in the planning stages of outreach and engagement activities, or when mapping who may currently be absent from a Citizen Observatory's network.

SOURCE: Nesta / Development Impact and You (DIY) Toolkit (2014, updated 2024). _People & Connections Map_. Based on the Stakeholder Mapping tool by Namahn and Yellow Window Service Design, Design Flanders (2012).
