# ASTC Community Science: Dialogue & Deliberation Toolkit

The Dialogue & Deliberation Toolkit was developed by the Association of Science and Technology Centers (ASTC) as part of their Community Science Initiative, with funding from the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative. It was co-created with science centre and museum practitioners through a 12-month fellowship programme and updated in 2023 based on their experiences. The toolkit is designed specifically for science engagement practitioners who want to use facilitated dialogue as a community science approach — bringing together community members to discuss issues at the intersection of science and society, surface shared priorities, and develop collaborative solutions.

The toolkit covers a range of facilitation methods and programme models drawn from civic engagement and science engagement practice, including public forums, World Cafés, conversation circles, intergroup dialogues, and study circles. It provides guidance on the foundational skills needed to plan and run a dialogue or deliberation initiative, and includes real-life examples from science centres and museums that have used these approaches to tackle issues such as climate change, access to green space, and affordable housing — all with a focus on elevating diverse voices and reaching underrepresented communities.
